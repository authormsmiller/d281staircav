      2d Bn., 8th Cav sets up FSB Fanning, just seven klicks from old FSB Fontaine. The new FSB has a better drainage system and new culvert hootches to shield the Monsoon rains. Transportation problems have been made easier also since the new base is only 200 meters from Quan Loi No. 1.
      
      The TOC and S-1 shops were moved intact to highlight a series of engineering feats. The move was accomplished by trucks and the base set up in three days but construction continues as improvements are made on living conditions and work sites. 1st Lt. Ronald Drooner, the S-3 air officer, proudly takes credit for anything that didn't go wrong.
      
      The moving, of course was not completed without a good deal of friendly hassling, which the 2d of the 8th interprets as a sign that things are going well. A cookout was planned to celebrate the move and christen the firebase (U.S. Army Photo by Pfc. Phil Seibert).
